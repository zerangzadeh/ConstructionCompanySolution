HR-065-01 — Plan/forecast sources

Extend the existing Project Controls forecast versions with an immutable
cash-allocation supplement reviewed through the existing forecast workflow,
including independent preparer/reviewer separation.

Each comparison explicitly selects an approved baseline supplement and an
approved forecast supplement. Each pins its accepted BudgetVersion, approved
ProjectCostForecast version, and applicable producer/commercial evidence.

Eligible supporting evidence is:

- accepted BudgetVersion/BudgetAllocation with pinned Estimate revision lineage;
- approved ProjectCostForecast and its retained source manifest;
- commitment facts under approved producer contracts, retaining source versions,
  adjustments and relief links;
- approved ContractTermVersion, Billing claim/certificate or AP/AR obligation
  evidence where applicable to a planned receipt or disbursement.

Each cash row records tenant/project, source family/UUID/version/line/hash,
economic-event identity, applicable contract/WBS/CBS context, direction
RECEIPT or DISBURSEMENT, positive Decimal cash_amount, source currency,
cash-basis evidence, and approval actor/time.

cash_amount is an explicitly reviewed planning amount.

Do not derive it automatically from:
- BudgetAllocation.amount
- EAC
- recognition amounts
- open commitment

Retain cost-to-cash differences and their supporting evidence.

Reconcile row counts and receipt/disbursement totals by source group and
currency to control totals approved with the supplement.

Do not perform FX conversion.

A previously approved forecast cannot acquire unreviewed cash rows.
New cash content requires a new reviewed forecast version.


HR-065-02 — Forecast timing

Every cash allocation requires:

`expected_cash_on`

as an ISO date explicitly approved with the allocation, together with its source
document/version and the source date-field or stated planning-assumption reference.

A contractual due date may be copied only when explicitly selected in that
approved allocation.

Do not infer cash dates automatically from:

- recognition date
- invoice date
- schedule start/finish
- milestone date
- payment_due_days

Missing dates remain explicit exceptions.

Overdue expected dates remain in their original buckets until an approved
successor changes them. Do not automatically roll them to today.


HR-065-03 — Reporting periods

Use Gregorian calendar-month buckets in the tenant
`OrganizationSettings.timezone`.

Retain the timezone in each snapshot.

Require explicit:

- `horizon_start`
- `horizon_end`

Both must be the first day of a month and:

`horizon_end > horizon_start`

The horizon and each bucket use:

[start inclusive, end exclusive)

No default horizon may be inferred.

Use identical boundaries for baseline, forecast and actual comparison.

Retain out-of-horizon amounts separately in reconciliation controls.


HR-065-04 — Allocation

Use explicit Decimal amount splits only.

For each source group and direction:

dated allocations
+
explicitly identified unallocated exceptions

must equal the approved cash control total exactly in the source currency.

Do not use:

- equal spreading
- duration weighting
- automatic percentage allocation
- automatic schedule-to-cash conversion

An allocation may reference an immutable schedule revision/item UUID and hash,
including draft schedule evidence, but its `expected_cash_on` requires independent
approval within the cash supplement.

This reference does NOT constitute schedule acceptance.

Identify overlapping commitment, invoice, obligation and forecast representations
using economic-event/source lineage.

The approved supplement selects one cash representation per event/allocation.
Related documents remain references.

Missing allocation, ambiguous overlap or unreconciled totals prevent a complete
comparison.

Partial results may remain visible only when their exceptions and coverage are
explicitly exposed.


HR-065-05 — Cutoff and corrections

Each snapshot requires a timezone-aware:

`knowledge_as_of`

timestamp no later than capture time.

Capture and retain source:

- versions
- approval/status history
- hashes
- reconciliation controls
- policy version

Plan/forecast inputs qualify only when recorded and approved by
`knowledge_as_of`.

Resolve their state from retained history at that timestamp.

Future `expected_cash_on` dates remain eligible when they fall within the
reporting horizon.

For actual cash:

- `posted_at <= knowledge_as_of`
- `movement_date` must be no later than the tenant-local date of
  `knowledge_as_of`

Use the approved Treasury actual-cash authority.

A later-recorded or later-approved backdated change enters only a subsequent
snapshot.

Its effect is placed in the bucket of its authoritative cash-event date,
including an earlier bucket when applicable.

For plan/forecast corrections, use the approved successor effective by the
knowledge cutoff instead of its predecessor.

An approved cancellation removes that planned contribution from subsequent
snapshots.

Retain the complete correction/replacement chain.

An existing snapshot must be rebuildable from its retained manifest and must
never be rebuilt from today's source pointers.

Missing historical state/version evidence blocks reconstruction.
