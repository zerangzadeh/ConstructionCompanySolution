### Historical timestamp handling

No historical rows were rewritten. A candidate report should restrict inspection to deployments and routes known to have used the affected naive form path, and to tenants whose **effective timezone at entry** was non-UTC. Cross-check the stored UTC wall clock against independent evidence of the intended local time, such as approved shift or attendance source, submission/audit chronology, and explicit offsets from trusted imports. Check the date boundary, existing lineage, and overlap/downstream effects. An offset-sized difference alone is insufficient. If authorized separately, run a read-only reconciliation first, preserve the source and audit evidence, and resolve confirmed cases through the applicable correction, reversal or governed supersession path. Cases with ambiguous evidence stay unresolved.
